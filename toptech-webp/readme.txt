One-click WebP for WooCommerce/WordPress. Install, activate, done.

== What it does ==
* Converts every JPEG and PNG in your Media Library to WebP (kept alongside the original).
* Auto-converts every new upload, including all thumbnail sizes.
* Serves WebP automatically to supporting browsers via auto-managed .htaccess rules. URLs stay .jpg/.png; the bytes delivered are WebP.
* Originals are never deleted. Deactivating removes the .htaccess rules and stops conversion; your images are untouched.

== How to use ==
1. Upload the zip under Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin, then Activate.
2. Go to Media > WebP Optimizer and click "Optimize all images now". It also runs in the background every few minutes.
